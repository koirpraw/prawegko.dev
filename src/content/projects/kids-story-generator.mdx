## Overview

The Kids Story Generator is an agentic AI pipeline that transforms simple prompts into fully-illustrated children's stories. It uses Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK) to orchestrate multiple specialized agents:

- **Story Weaver** — Generates narrative arcs, characters, and plot
- **Illustration Engine** — Creates scene descriptions and prompts for image generation
- **Formatting Agent** — Composes the final output with proper layout and styling
- **Review Agent** — Ensures age-appropriate content and narrative coherence

### Architecture

```
User Prompt
    |
    v
[Story Weaver] ---- generates narrative ----> [Review Agent]
    |                                                |
    v                                                v
[Illustration Engine]                       Feedback Loop
    |
    v
[Formatting Agent]
    |
    v
Final Story (MDX + Images)
```

### Technical Details

- **Framework:** Google ADK (Agent Development Kit)
- **LLM:** Gemini 2.5 Pro (primary), Gemini 2.5 Flash (review agent)
- **Image Gen:** Gemini-native image generation
- **Format:** MDX output with embedded illustrations
